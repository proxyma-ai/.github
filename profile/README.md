## Proxyma

Self-hosted AI that answers from your file shares, Jira and Confluence - with citations,
on your own machine, with the model you choose.

- **[proxyma.ai](https://proxyma.ai)** - download, docs, pricing
- **[proxyma-community](https://github.com/proxyma-ai/proxyma-community)** - the source, AGPL-3.0
- **[proxyma-releases](https://github.com/proxyma-ai/proxyma-releases)** - installers and packages

Built and maintained by [Le Sy Hau](https://github.com/lesyhau), an engineer in Vietnam.
